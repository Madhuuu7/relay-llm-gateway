# Fonts served with the console

Two typefaces are served from `web/public/fonts` beside the bundle rather than
fetched from a font host. This console is demoed on a free instance that has
just woken up, and a typeface that arrives late is a page in the wrong face at
exactly the wrong moment. Latin subsets only, about 95 KB in total.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which permits
this. Their copyright notices, as required:

- **Spline Sans** — Copyright © 2021 The Spline Sans Project Authors.
  <https://github.com/SorkinType/SplineSans>
  A technical grotesque with narrow, unambiguous figures, which is most of
  what this sheet is made of.
- **Spline Sans Mono** — Copyright © 2022 The Spline Sans Mono Project
  Authors. <https://github.com/SorkinType/SplineSansMono>
  Holds the columns that have to align. On this page a monospaced figure
  means a measurement.

The licence text is at <https://openfontlicense.org/>. Neither font is
modified; the files are the Google Fonts latin subsets.
